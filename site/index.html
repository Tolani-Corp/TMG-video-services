    Operating principles

    Capability never outruns authority.

    • Rights before retrieval.Source authority and permitted purpose are evaluated before content is released.
    • Lineage by default.Media, derivatives, and retrieval outputs remain bound to provenance and integrity evidence.
    • Human release gates.Technical readiness does not automatically grant publication, provider, billing, or commercial authority.
